### Background 
Federated query processing aims to collect data from multiple databases. In the dice research group, we have an engine named CostFed that performs the federated SPARQL query processing over multipl Knowledge Graphs. 
It performs the traditional binary join approach, i.e., takes two SPARQL triple pattern and make a join of the results. The results are then joined with another triple pattern, untill all the triple patterns are joined and final results are computed for the given query.  
In this thesis, we aim to extended the query processing capabilities of Costfed to perform the multi-way joins, i.e., take more than one triple pattern at a time and perform a join, untill left no join variable. 
### Your Task
